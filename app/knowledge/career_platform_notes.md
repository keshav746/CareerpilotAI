# CareerPilot platform interpretation

A job skill-match score indicates overlap between a student's listed skills and a job listing's required skills. It is not the probability of receiving an offer. Missing skills are learning suggestions, not evidence that a student is unqualified for every role.

CareerPilot includes illustrative sample listings and may include recruiter-submitted listings. Neither type guarantees a vacancy is currently open. Students should verify jobs with employers before applying. CareerPilot does not automatically submit external job applications.
